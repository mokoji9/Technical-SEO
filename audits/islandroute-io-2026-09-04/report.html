What's helping and hurting Island Route in search

A live technical crawl of the Island Route marketing site — the self-guided Mauritius audio tour app — checked against crawlability, indexing, performance, and structured-data fundamentals.

At a glance

The fundamentals are in good shape — fast load times, clean markup, correct canonical and viewport tags. But one issue overshadows the rest: the site's Cloudflare bot protection is inconsistently challenging or outright blocking automated requests, including a spoofed Googlebot user-agent. If the real Googlebot is affected, none of the other wins here matter — the site can't rank if it can't be crawled. That gets verified and fixed first; everything below it is real, but secondary.

Findings

Ordered by severity — how much each issue could be costing in search visibility.

Critical Crawlability Quick win once confirmed

Cloudflare bot protection may be blocking search crawlers

Several independent checks all pointed the same direction: something in front of the site is treating automated, non-browser requests as suspicious — sometimes serving a flat block, sometimes an interactive "Verify you are human" challenge that a browser clears automatically but a crawler cannot.

curl -A "Googlebot/2.1" https://www.islandroute.io/
→ 403 Forbidden
curl -A "Chrome/120.0 (desktop browser)" https://www.islandroute.io/
→ 200 OK
DataForSEO's on-page crawler (JS rendering on): 403 Forbidden
Live browser session, first visit: served Cloudflare's "Just a moment…" challenge page (noindex,nofollow) — cleared automatically on retry

Googlebot can't solve interactive challenges. If it's hitting this one, pages quietly stop getting (re)crawled — no warning, no error email, just fading rankings.

Before changing anything: a spoofed curl user-agent isn't proof the real Googlebot is blocked — Cloudflare should distinguish them by IP/rDNS. Confirm first with Search Console → URL Inspection → Test Live URL, and check the Crawl Stats report for a drop in successful requests.
Fix guidefixes/crawlability/cloudflare-bot-protection-blocking-crawlers.md
High On-page content Moderate

51 of 54 homepage images are missing alt text

Only 3 of the 54 <img> elements on the homepage have descriptive alt attributes. This is the main reason the Lighthouse Accessibility score sits at 85 instead of higher, and it means Google (and screen readers) have almost no idea what most of the page's imagery — including the destination photography that sells the product — is actually showing.

NextAdd descriptive alt text sitewide, prioritizing the homepage gallery and app screenshots
Medium Indexability Quick win

/old-home is thin placeholder content, live in the sitemap

The page's entire content is "THE TREASURE HUNT IS STARTING SOON" and a sign-up button — it reads like an abandoned test page, not a real one. It's still listed in sitemap.xml, which actively tells Google to crawl and index it alongside real pages.

Decision needed301 redirect it, noindex it, or drop it from the sitemap
Medium Structured data Moderate

Only one schema type is in use (WebSite)

The homepage carries a single WebSite JSON-LD block and nothing else. Given the site already has /faq and /mauritius-faq pages, both are strong, low-effort candidates for FAQPage schema and a shot at rich results — plus an Organization block sitewide for brand recognition.

Fix guidefixes/structured-data/missing-schema-markup.md
Low Crawlability Moderate — dev task

Every sitemap URL shares the same lastmod date

All 13 URLs in sitemap.xml — from the homepage to the Terms & Conditions page — carry the identical lastmod value. It suggests the sitemap is a static, hand-generated file rather than reflecting real update history, which slightly undercuts its usefulness as a freshness signal.

Suggestion Emerging

Lighthouse's "Agentic Browsing" score sits at 50/100

A newer Lighthouse category measuring how well a page works for AI browsing agents. Not an established ranking factor yet — worth a glance later, not a priority now.

What's already working

No action needed here — worth knowing what not to touch.

  • ✓robots.txt is clean and correctly points to the sitemap
  • ✓Title, meta description, canonical & viewport tags all present and correct
  • ✓HTTPS is enforced across the site
  • ✓Mobile layout renders cleanly at 375×812
  • ✓Core Web Vitals all comfortably within Google's "good" thresholds
  • ✓Lighthouse SEO & Best Practices both score a perfect 100

Recommended order of attack

  1. Verify and fix the Cloudflare issue first. Everything else is secondary until this is confirmed clean — it's the one issue that could be silently erasing search visibility outright.
  2. Add alt text across the homepage image set. Straightforward content fix; lifts both accessibility and image search visibility.
  3. Decide the fate of /old-home. Redirect, noindex, or remove from the sitemap — needs a quick call from whoever owns the content.
  4. Expand structured data. FAQPage schema on the two FAQ pages is the highest-ROI addition.
  5. Automate the sitemap's lastmod dates. Low priority — fold into a future dev sprint.
